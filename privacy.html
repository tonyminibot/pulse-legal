Pulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

Pulse is a revenue-analytics app for your own Paddle account. It is designed to be private by default.

What we collect

Nothing. Pulse has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or usage data.

Your Paddle API key

The read-only Paddle API key you enter is stored only in your device's iOS Keychain. It is never sent anywhere except directly from your device to Paddle's official API (api.paddle.com) over HTTPS to fetch your own revenue and transaction data.

Your data

Revenue and transaction data fetched from Paddle is cached only on your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear the cache in Settings. It is never shared with us or any third party.
