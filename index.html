Your Paddle revenue, at a glance

Pulse turns your Paddle account into a clean, private revenue dashboard — today, this week, this month — combined across every account, right on your iPhone.

Download on the App Store How it works
Revenue dashboard

Today, 7-day & 30-day — vs the period before

Big, clear numbers with a live trend chart and a dashed comparison line. Scrub any point. Switch between All, One-off and Renewal revenue instantly.

Transactions

Every sale, searchable — with the detail that matters

Filter by product, search by customer or id, and tap any sale for gross, tax, Paddle fee, net, customer and country.

Home-screen widgets

Revenue without opening the app

Small and medium widgets show today, 7-day and 30-day revenue with deltas, refreshed automatically. Glance and go.

Multiple accounts

Every Paddle account, combined into one view

Add as many read-only keys as you run. Revenue, transactions, KPIs and charts are merged into a single, unified picture.

Private by design

Your key never leaves your iPhone

No servers. No accounts. No tracking. Your read-only Paddle key is stored in the iOS Keychain and talks straight to Paddle.

On-device only

Data is cached on your phone and cleared when you delete the app.

Read-only

Pulse only ever reads. It can never change anything in your Paddle account.

Gross, done right

Revenue is post-discount, pre-tax & fee — the number you actually care about.
